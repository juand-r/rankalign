The Generator–Validator Gap

Language models can be probed for the same knowledge in two ways.

Example: hypernymy ("a bee is a kind of    ").

Generator
"A bee is a kind of ___" → look at next-token probabilities.
Validator
"Is a bee a kind of furniture? Answer: ___" → look at Yes/No log-odds.

A well-calibrated model should rank these consistently — but often it doesn't.

When Does Typicality Correction Help?

For correct y* and incorrect yn, define the corrected score gap:

A = raw score gap  |  B = correction gap  |  want D > 0

B < 0  (correct answer less typical)

Correction helps — want large α.

B > 0  (correct answer more typical)

Correction hurts — want small α.

Examples (IFEval):

Star Wars bullet points

Wrong answers are longer (too many bullets, paragraphs).
b(yn) < b(y*)  ⇒  B > 0.  TC with α=1 hurts.

Math undershoot

Correct answers are longer (less typical).
b(y*) < b(yn)  ⇒  B < 0.
TC with α=1 helps.
