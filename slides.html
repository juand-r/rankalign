The Generator–Validator Gap

Language models can be probed for the same knowledge in two ways.

Example: hypernymy ("a bee is a kind of    ").

Generator
"A bee is a kind of ___" → look at next-token probabilities.
Validator
"Is a bee a kind of furniture? Answer: ___" → look at Yes/No log-odds.

A well-calibrated model should rank these consistently — but often it doesn't.

Li et al., Benchmarking and Improving Generator-Validator Consistency of Language Models. ICLR 2024
